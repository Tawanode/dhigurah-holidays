ARRIVAL & TRANSFER GUIDANCE

Domestic Flight + Speedboat

A scenic 20-minute domestic flight from Velana International Airport (MLE) to Maamigili Airport (VAM), followed by our dedicated 20-minute guesthouse speedboat transfer across the lagoon directly to Dhigurah jetty.

Direct Scheduled Speedboat

Direct sea transfers departing daily from Malé harbor straight to Dhigurah (approx. 1 hour 45 minutes, weather permitting). We manage ticket bookings on your behalf.

ESSENTIAL ISLAND LOGISTICS

Helpful Notes & Local Island Protocol

Dhigurah is an inhabited Maldivian island. Experiencing authentic local culture goes hand in hand with responsible, tranquil travel habits.

apparel

Island Dress Codes

Inside the local village and public streets, modest attire covering shoulders and knees is respected by local customs. On Dhigurah's dedicated white Bikini Beach and excursion boats, swimwear and standard resort beach attire are fully welcomed.

Bikini Beach Access: Free & 5-min walk
schedule

Transfer Scheduling

Speedboats operate on set timetables (typically morning and afternoon runs from Malé). To secure transfers seamlessly, we recommend providing international arrival flight numbers at least 3 days prior to your arrival date.

Lagoon Transit: 1hr 45m / Air: 20m + boat
payments

Payment & Currencies

We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard) as well as cash payments in USD (crisp, unblemished banknotes series 2013+) and Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR). A local Bank of Maldives ATM is situated on the island for cash withdrawals.

On-Island ATM: Active in village center
