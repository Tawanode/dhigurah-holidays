CHAPTER 02

Our Setting & Island Geography

In Dhivehi, Dhigurah translates directly to "Long Island"—a fitting name for one of the most distinctive geological formations in the Maldives. The island stretches uninterrupted for over three kilometers, yet measures barely three hundred meters across at its widest point.

Dense green jungle trails run the spine of the island, shaded by ancient sea hibiscus, banyan, and coconut palms. Walking or cycling south reveals miles of secluded white-sand beaches, culminating at the iconic tapering sandbank where calm lagoon waters meet the open ocean reef.

CHAPTER 04 • CONSERVATION SAMPA ZONE

Guardians of South Ari Reefs

WHALE SHARK AGGREGATION • SOUTH ARI ATOLL

Dhigurah is situated along the South Ari Marine Protected Area (SAMPA), globally celebrated as one of the few places on Earth where whale sharks reside year-round.

We view this marine abundance not as an attraction to exploit, but as a living sanctuary to safeguard. We advocate strict codes of conduct: maintaining safe physical distances from marine life, prohibiting touching or path obstruction, and using only reef-safe mineral sunscreen during all ocean excursions.

  • — Zero direct contact policy with whale sharks and manta rays.
  • — Strict encouragement of certified reef-safe mineral sunscreens.
  • — Support for local beach and lagoon cleanup initiatives.
CHAPTER 05 • ETIQUETTE COMMUNITY HARMONY

Local Culture & Island Etiquette

VILLAGE HARMONY & LOCAL COMMERCE

Staying on a local island is an enriching cultural privilege. Dhigurah is an active community with sacred traditions, family homes, and religious values that have sustained this atoll for centuries.

We invite our guests to honor local life. Within village boundaries, modest attire covering shoulders and knees is practiced by both men and women. For swimwear and sunbathing, Dhigurah boasts designated expansive Bikini Beaches where guests can freely lounge along miles of pristine shore.

  • — Modest attire within the village center and residential pathways.
  • — Dedicated designated Bikini Beach for uninterrupted swim and sun.
  • — Direct support of independent island cafes, grocery shops, and artisans.
