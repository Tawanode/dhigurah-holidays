02 / OCEAN GIANTS

Whale Shark Excursions

Dhigurah lies immediately adjacent to the South Ari Marine Protected Area (SAMPA), widely recognized as the preeminent location globally for year-round whale shark encounters. We practice strict low-impact marine observation, ensuring animals are never crowded, fed, or disrupted.

NATURAL CONSERVATION MANDATE

Sightings are completely wild and natural; nature guides our journeys and wildlife encounters are never forced or guaranteed. Each journey is accompanied by our resident naturalist.

04 / THE NATURAL PROMONTORY

The Southern Sandbank

At the southernmost tip of Dhigurah, the land tapers into an iconic, pristine sand spit stretching hundreds of meters straight into the azure Indian Ocean. Submerged only during high seasonal spring tides, this geological marvel provides an unmatched sense of absolute solitude.

  • 01 Scenic 45-minute coastal walking route along the uninhabited jungle flank.
  • 02 Uninterrupted 360-degree panoramas of emerald shoals and oceanic horizons.
  • 03 Bespoke sunset picnics and quiet reading afternoons away from all settlements.

CULTURAL HARMONY & REEF PROTECTION

Responsible Travel & Island Etiquette

Dhigurah is a living, working Maldivian community, not a privatized commercial resort island. We take deep pride in preserving both our vibrant cultural heritage and our vulnerable coral ecosystems.

01

Village Dress Codes

When walking within the inhabited island village, please respect local Islamic traditions by ensuring shoulders and knees are modestly covered. Swimwear is warmly welcomed on dedicated Bikini Beaches.

02

Marine Conservation

Never touch coral heads, stand on shallow reefs, or chase marine wildlife. We mandate reef-safe mineral sunscreens exclusively to prevent toxic chemical leaching into delicate lagoon habitats.

03

Local Economy Support

Our excursions are directly captained and guided by Dhigurah islanders. We encourage patronizing locally owned craft ateliers, bakeries, and corner cafés to ensure tourism directly benefits our neighbors.
