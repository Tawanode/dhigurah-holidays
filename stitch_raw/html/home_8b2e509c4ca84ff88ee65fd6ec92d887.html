WELCOME TO DHIGURAH

A Little Closer to the Real Maldives

Stretching over three kilometers along the rim of the South Ari Atoll, Dhigurah is celebrated for its endless powder-soft sandbanks, untouched tropical jungle spine, and celebrated marine sanctuary.

At Dhigurah Holidays, we curate an intimate refuge away from artificial resort complexes. Here, island hospitality flows at the steady tempo of ocean tides, salt breeze, and morning fishing skiffs drifting into the lagoon.

AUTHENTIC MALDIVES

On Dhigurah, in the Heart of South Ari Atoll

Unlike artificial resort islands isolated behind seawalls, Dhigurah is an active, welcoming Maldivian island community. Here, life is grounded in respectful island traditions, local boat craftsmanship, and a gentle rhythm shaped by sunrise and sunset.

Stroll through quiet, vehicle-free sand alleys shaded by breadfruit trees, exchange warm hellos with resident islanders, and sip fresh coconut water while watching the fishermen return at dusk.

About Dhigurah Holidays

We believe the real charm of the Maldives belongs to its living local islands, where nature remains untamed and hospitality is personal.

Dhigurah Holidays was founded to bridge understated modern comfort with authentic local encounters. Located in Dhigurah village, we invite travellers who seek meaningful connections: waking up to bird calls in the palm canopy, diving with majestic pelagic wildlife, and supporting the resident island economy directly.

Our dedicated team lives right here on Dhigurah, eager to recommend quiet swim spots, arrange domestic sea-plane or boat connections from Malé, and ensure your stay is as carefree as the island breeze.

TRANSFERS TO DHIGURAH

Scheduled speedboat departs daily from Malé Airport direct to Dhigurah harbor (approx. 1h 45m). Flyme domestic flights operate between Velana International (MLE) and nearby Maamigili (VAM) with 20-minute boat connection.
